Lector site — light mode, six alternatives

Same idea as the dark mode you picked: pull the two ends toward each other instead of pinning them at #ffffff and #111111, and let --line carry the hairlines so borders stop reading as text. Chrome below is the real ledger skin — 1 px hairlines, square corners, no shadows. Ratios are text-on-paper; today's light mode is 16.1:1.
